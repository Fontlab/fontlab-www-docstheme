# FontLab brand and product assets

Download original wordmarks, banners, app icons and screenshots for FontLab
products, partnerships and press coverage.

- [Company wordmarks](fontlab-marketingpack-company.md): editable logo font,
  Illustrator sources, PDFs and PNGs in the supplied black, gray and white variants.
- [Product banners](fontlab-banners.md): four 728 × 90 campaign themes in PDF and PNG.
- [FontLab 8](fontlab-marketingpack-fontlab-8.md): app icons, hero and slide headers,
  screenshots and store copy.
- [TransType 4](fontlab-marketingpack-transtype-4.md): app icons, screenshots and
  composed screenshot layouts.

Each pack lists every original file with format and size. Assets previously
published by `fontlabcom/fontlab-design-assets` now live in this catalogue.

## Using the assets

These assets belong to Fontlab Ltd. Use them to represent FontLab products and
partnerships. Keep the wordmark intact: no stretching or recolouring beyond the
provided tints. Ask before adapting an asset to a missing format or size.

For usage questions or a missing asset, open an issue in the
[Marketing repository](https://github.com/fontlab/Marketing/issues).
